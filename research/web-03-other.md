# FUNAAB-named domains and independent resources

**Research scope.** This report covers independently discoverable pages whose domain or title contains FUNAAB, plus linked/public academic resources encountered during the search. It does not bypass login, paywalls, robots rules, or access controls. URLs are deduplicated. Official university sources are ranked above third-party resources. “FUNAAB 101” is explicitly treated as an independent student guide, not as an official university publication.

## Past-question and study-note resources

The independent [CropPSA notes mirror](http://croppsa.blogspot.com/p/my-notes.html) lists first-year notes such as ANP 101, CHM 101/191/102/104/192, MTS 105/106, PCP 101/191, PHS 105/191/106/192, GNS 101/102/103; second-year entries such as AEM 201/204, AGE 222/223, CSC 211, FST 201, FWM 201, PCP 201/202, PRM 201, SOS 211, STS 201, ANN 202, APH 202, ARD 202 and WMA 202; and selected third-year entries such as AEM 302/304, APH 302, CPT 302, FWM 314, SOS 312/314, PBS 302 and AGE 322. It distinguishes “single format” notes from compressed semester archives. No direct PDF URL was exposed in the fetched page, so no guessed file URL is included.

The official OpenCourseWare index itself labels some resources as “Past Questions,” “Tutorial,” “Sample Examination” or “Continuous Assessment Test,” including ANP 201 Past Questions, ANP 502 Sample Examination, and related lecture/assessment items. The dedicated official Computer Past Questions and FST Examination Questions pages were found, but their public rendered text did not expose direct downloadable question-file URLs. The FST page explicitly shows 500 Level and 700 Level. The independent Scholarly Africa page describes a FUNAAB Post-UTME Past Questions & Answers offline resource, but the public page does not expose an unrestricted PDF.

## Direct public PDF URLs

Only one clearly identified, publicly accessible PDF URL was verified during this pass:

1. [FUNAAB Postgraduate Advert 2025/2026 (PDF)](https://pg.unaab.edu.ng/files/Advert_PG_20252026.pdf) — official postgraduate-school document. The fetched PDF is publicly reachable and contains postgraduate admission/programme information; it is not a course-note or past-question repository.

Search results also surfaced third-party documents with FUNAAB in snippets (including Scribd, PDFCoffee and unrelated document hosts), but no direct unrestricted FUNAAB course-note/past-question PDF was verified from those results. They are omitted rather than treated as authoritative or guessed. The repository and OpenCourseWare pages may link to file assets dynamically; because the fetched public HTML did not expose stable direct PDF URLs, those assets are reported as pages rather than fabricated file links.

## Caveats

The official university pages are substantially more reliable than independent mirrors and commercial/third-party study products. Course titles and synopsis text are transcribed only where displayed by the fetched source; spelling irregularities in the source (for example, “isomometric,” “couphlings,” or typographical errors in the PHS page) are not silently corrected as substantive claims. FUNAAB 101 expressly describes itself as independent and instructs users to verify fees, deadlines and official decisions with the university. A page titled “past questions” does not by itself prove that downloadable questions remain publicly accessible, so no such files are claimed where the fetched page did not expose them.
