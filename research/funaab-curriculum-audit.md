# FUNAAB consolidated curriculum evidence synthesis

This synthesis consolidates the eight supplied college audits into a deduplicated, source-backed dataset. It preserves the audits’ evidence boundaries: a row appears only when an official FUNAAB source cited by the audit publishes the course code/title (and units where available). `unknown` status means the cited source does not identify the row as compulsory or elective; it is not a guess.

**Dataset totals:** 49 programme records; **1488 deduplicated rows**; **25 documented gaps**.

## Interpretation and gaps

- **Agriculture:** the official calendar publishes a shared 100–400 B.Agric. curriculum and option-specific 500-level tables only for Agricultural Administration, Agricultural Economics and Farm Management, and Agricultural Extension and Rural Development. The other ten options retain shared rows but have no invented 500-level rows. The broad manifest label “Agriculture” is not counted as a separate option.
- **Engineering:** official course-list/synopsis pages provide uneven departmental rows. Agricultural, Civil and Mechanical include some 100-coded rows, but no complete common engineering 100-level table or row-level compulsory/elective evidence was located; Mechatronics has programme evidence but no course rows.
- **Environmental:** EMT rows begin at 300 and WMA/WRM rows at 200. No 100-level EMT/WMA/common COLERM table was located. Three related admission titles are retained as programme-level gaps rather than copying WMA rows across them.
- **Food:** Hospitality and Tourism is the only food-area programme with published 100-level/common rows. FST and HSM synopsis coverage begins at 200; NTD begins with NTD 103 in the source discussion but its tabulated rows begin at 200. No complete current handbook was located.
- **Management:** Business Administration, Economics and Entrepreneurial Studies have levelled 100–400 calendar tables; Banking and Finance has only a published 400-level table. Accounting, Cooperative Studies, Development Studies and Library and Information Science have programme evidence but no complete public levelled table.
- **Computing:** only historical Computer Science synopsis rows were publishable; their level is explicitly not stated in the source, so numeric codes were not converted to levels. The six other named programmes have no course rows in the reviewed official sources.
- **Physical sciences:** Chemistry, Mathematics and Physics include 100-level-coded synopsis rows; Statistics begins at 200 in the published synopsis. Industrial Chemistry and Geophysics are officially named but have no course lists in the reviewed sources. No COLPHYS-wide common-course table was located.
- **Veterinary Medicine:** only VCM 102 is publicly published at 100 level; the College confirms a pre-professional 100-level phase but does not publish a complete 100-level table. Professional-phase (200–600) rows are marked compulsory under the College rule. No elective rows were verified.

### Status evidence

The dataset contains **798 compulsory**, **100 elective**, and **590 unknown-status rows**. Agriculture and management explicitly label elective blocks; Veterinary explicitly establishes professional-phase compulsory status; Computing explicitly labels only CSC 201 and CSC 426 compulsory. Engineering, Environmental, Food and Physical Sciences generally publish course rows without row-level status, so their statuses remain unknown.

### Source and completeness caveat

Official department/course pages and calendars are authoritative for the rows they publish, but several are legacy synopsis/courseware pages rather than a current Senate-approved semester registration handbook. The JSON `gaps` array records missing 100-level/common-course evidence, missing exact-title crosswalks, missing row-level status, and missing programme tables.
