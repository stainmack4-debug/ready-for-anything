# FUNAAB Academic Domain Research: Entrepreneurial, Development and Library Studies

**Scope.** This report covers the eight programmes listed by FUNAAB under the 2026/2027 admission grouping **Entrepreneurial and Development Studies**: Accounting; Banking and Finance; Business Administration; Cooperative Studies; Development Studies; Economics; Entrepreneurial Studies; and Library and Information Science. The catalogue synopses below are programme-level descriptions only; they do not infer a course-unit syllabus from isolated course notes.

## Institutional context

FUNAAB’s official [College of Entrepreneurial and Development Studies page](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/college-of-entrepreneurial-and-development-studies/) says the college was formerly the College of Management Sciences, was established in 2011, and had NUC-accredited programmes in Accounting, Banking and Finance, Business Administration, Economics and Entrepreneurial Studies. It states that the college was renamed in 2021 to reflect Development Studies-related expansion. The college’s stated philosophy is to combine theoretical and practical knowledge, self-confidence, innovation and self-reliance; its mission emphasises relevant curricula, stakeholders, industry and national policy.

The current [FUNAAB 2026/2027 admissions programme list](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/programmes.php) is the authoritative source located for the exact eight-item list and shows minimum UTME scores: Accounting (200), Banking and Finance (200), Business Administration (200), Cooperative Studies (160), Development Studies (160), Economics (200), Entrepreneurial Studies (180), and Library and Information Science (180). These marks are admission-screening thresholds, not curriculum descriptions.

## Programme catalogue entries

### 1. Accounting

**Synopsis.** FUNAAB describes Accounting as preparing efficient and effective accounting scholars and practitioners, treating the discipline as a hub for public- and private-sector activity. Its objectives include globally competitive graduates, teaching/professional/service careers, ethical discipline, sensitivity to socio-economic and environmental influences on accounting, and teamwork, interpersonal and leadership skills. This is a programme-level philosophy/objectives summary; no course list is asserted.

**Official sources:** [Department of Accounting](https://funaab.edu.ng/college-of-management-sciences-colmas/department-of-accounting/); [College of Entrepreneurial and Development Studies](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/college-of-entrepreneurial-and-development-studies/); [2026/2027 admissions programme list](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/programmes.php).

**Direct PDFs:** [FUNAAB Admission Requirements 2026 PDF](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/files/FUNAABAdmissionRequirements.pdf).

**Confidence:** High for the philosophy/objectives and programme listing. **Gaps:** No official Accounting curriculum/handbook or unit-by-unit syllabus was located in the reviewed FUNAAB pages. The admission PDF is an entry-requirements document, not a curriculum.

### 2. Banking and Finance

**Synopsis.** FUNAAB frames Banking and Finance around finance concepts and theories needed to address complex financial and economic issues and improve decisions by government, corporations and individuals. The programme also stresses self-reliance and ethical conduct in financial services, and aims to develop professional/future leadership capability in finance and general management, preparation for professional or graduate study, teamwork and enterprise-building.

**Official sources:** [Department of Banking and Finance](https://funaab.edu.ng/college-of-management-sciences-colmas/department-of-banking-and-finance/); [College of Entrepreneurial and Development Studies](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/college-of-entrepreneurial-and-development-studies/); [2026/2027 admissions programme list](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/programmes.php).

**Direct PDFs:** [FUNAAB Admission Requirements 2026 PDF](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/files/FUNAABAdmissionRequirements.pdf); [BFN 403 Quantitative Analysis for Financial Decisions PDF](https://funaab.edu.ng/wp-content/uploads/sixth.pdf) (official course-unit document, not evidence of the whole programme curriculum).

**Confidence:** High for programme philosophy/objectives. **Gaps:** The located BFN PDF is a single course-unit document and must not be treated as a full curriculum; no complete official handbook was found.

### 3. Business Administration

**Synopsis.** FUNAAB’s programme philosophy combines theoretical and practical management knowledge with self-reliance and the ability to understand, exploit and potentially change the environment through management theories, principles, processes and techniques. Objectives cover analysing management problems in industrial, commercial, public and other organisations; scientific decision tools; environmental awareness; leadership and interpersonal relations; entrepreneurship; manager, teacher and researcher preparation; and Nigerian-context management research and materials.

**Official sources:** [Department of Business Administration](https://funaab.edu.ng/college-of-management-sciences-colmas/department-of-business-administration/); [College of Entrepreneurial and Development Studies](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/college-of-entrepreneurial-and-development-studies/); [2026/2027 admissions programme list](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/programmes.php).

**Direct PDFs:** [FUNAAB Admission Requirements 2026 PDF](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/files/FUNAABAdmissionRequirements.pdf).

**Confidence:** High for programme philosophy/objectives. **Gaps:** No complete official curriculum, course handbook or unit syllabi located.

### 6. Economics

**Synopsis.** FUNAAB presents Economics as developing theoretical and practical knowledge for graduates to function in academia, industry and public-sector settings nationally and internationally. The programme objective is to produce graduates with a strong academic foundation and the skills to handle contemporary economic issues and policies, specifically through economic theory, tools of economic analysis, and useful knowledge and skills for graduates and society. The department also states a mission of knowledge generation through research, teaching, learning and community outreach toward sustainable development.

**Official sources:** [Department of Economics](https://funaab.edu.ng/college-of-management-sciences-colmas/department-of-economics/); [Department of Economics section page](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/department-of-ecomomics/); [College of Entrepreneurial and Development Studies](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/college-of-entrepreneurial-and-development-studies/); [2026/2027 admissions programme list](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/programmes.php).

### 7. Entrepreneurial Studies

**Synopsis.** FUNAAB describes Entrepreneurial Studies as developing innovative, creative and entrepreneurial personalities prepared for leadership in industry, government and non-profit organisations. The stated objectives include entrepreneurial zeal and knowledge, viable globally competitive enterprises, opportunity recognition and exploitation, growth of new and existing ventures, productive culture, advisory/mentoring engagement, and analytical skills in problem solving, negotiation, conflict resolution, marketing, leadership, interpersonal relations and financial management. It also prepares graduates for further study.

**Official sources:** [Department of Entrepreneurial Studies](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/department-of-entrepreneurial-studies/); [legacy Department of Entrepreneurial Studies page](https://funaab.edu.ng/college-of-management-sciences-colmas/department-of-entrepreneurial-studies/); [College of Entrepreneurial and Development Studies](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/college-of-entrepreneurial-and-development-studies/); [2026/2027 admissions programme list](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/programmes.php).

**Direct PDFs:** [FUNAAB Admission Requirements 2026 PDF](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/files/FUNAABAdmissionRequirements.pdf).

**Confidence:** High for philosophy/objectives and programme listing. **Gaps:** No complete official curriculum or course handbook located. The college page notes degree options and later development-oriented expansion but does not publish a course-by-course structure.

### 8. Library and Information Science

**Synopsis.** FUNAAB’s official programme proposal describes Library and Information Science as training professionals who can develop/apply relevant technologies and information, manage information in all formats, and work in physical and virtual libraries and related information organisations. Objectives include professional preparation for libraries, information/documentation centres; theoretical and practical competence; inquiry and creativity; adaptability; and understanding new communication technologies such as the Internet. The page identifies potential fields including academic, school, national, public, research and special libraries; ICT and related industries; archives, museums, information-management/curation centres; and public establishments.

**Official sources:** [Department of Library and Information Studies](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/department-of-library-and-information-studies/); [FUNAAB announcement of NUC approval for new programmes](https://funaab.edu.ng/nuc-approves-eight-new-programmes-for-funaab/); [College of Entrepreneurial and Development Studies](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/college-of-entrepreneurial-and-development-studies/); [2026/2027 admissions programme list](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/programmes.php).

**Direct PDFs:** [FUNAAB Admission Requirements 2026 PDF](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/files/FUNAABAdmissionRequirements.pdf).

**Confidence:** High for the programme philosophy/objectives and existence. **Gaps:** No complete official Library and Information Science curriculum or handbook located. FUNAAB’s NUC announcement lists B.LIS among eight newly approved programmes commencing in 2024/2025, while the later admissions page uses the name “Library and Information Science”; the naming difference should be retained as a catalogue alias rather than treated as two programmes.

## Research gaps and source policy

No secondary student guide was used. Official FUNAAB web pages were available for five programmes (Accounting, Banking and Finance, Business Administration, Economics and Entrepreneurial Studies), and official admission listings were available for all eight. Cooperative Studies and Development Studies are therefore represented only at the programme-list/college-context level. No publicly accessible complete undergraduate curriculum or course handbook was located for the eight programmes during this review. Individual course-unit PDFs, where found, are explicitly labelled and are not generalised into programme content.
