# FUNAAB Academic Domain Research: Engineering

**Scope.** This report covers the five Engineering programmes listed by the Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta (FUNAAB) under Engineering for the 2026/2027 admission cycle: Agricultural Engineering, Civil Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, and Mechatronic Engineering. Sources were prioritized in this order: official FUNAAB programme/department pages, official FUNAAB admission pages, official FUNAAB course-synopsis pages, and official FUNAAB PDFs. No secondary student guide was needed.

## Domain-level evidence

FUNAAB's official [2026/2027 programme listing](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/programmes.php) places all five programmes under **Engineering**, each with a displayed minimum acceptable UTME score of 200. The official [College of Engineering overview](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/college-of-engineering/) describes instruction through classroom teaching, laboratory practicals, field demonstrations, and workshop practice. The older [College of Engineering page](https://funaab.edu.ng/college-of-engineering-coleng/) explicitly describes five-year B.Eng. programmes, but its historical department list predates the current Mechatronic Engineering listing; therefore, the current admission page and current Mechatronics department page are used for that programme's current presence.

The official course-synopsis pages are **course-unit-level references**, not complete programme descriptions or necessarily current curriculum handbooks. They are useful for catalogue grounding, but the synopses should not be presented as a complete, current semester-by-semester curriculum. For example, the Agricultural page identifies AGE units such as engineering drawing, workshop practice, industrial experience, farm mechanics, hydraulics, farm electrification, agricultural materials processing, structures, farm machinery and farm power; the Civil page identifies CVE units including Engineer in Society, Strength of Materials, SWEP and SIWES; the Electrical page identifies circuit theory, electromagnetic theory, electrical machines, electronic circuits, power, control and communications; the Mechanical page identifies mechanics, materials, thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, machine design, manufacturing and instrumentation; and the Mechatronics page identifies CAD/CAM/CNC, control, instrumentation, computer hardware, power electronics, robotics, PLC/process automation, embedded systems and renewable energy. These examples are evidence of individual course-unit scope, not a claim that they are the entire current programme.

## Programme catalogue entries

### Agricultural Engineering

**Synopsis.** FUNAAB describes the programme as producing engineering manpower able to provide scientific and engineering solutions to agricultural, bioresources and ecological problems, with goals including agricultural productivity, environmental sustainability, poverty reduction and entrepreneurship. The stated objectives cover design of systems, processes and machinery for crop, livestock and fibre production/storage; design, manufacture and testing of machines for biological-material processing and storage; ecological problem-solving; irrigation, drainage, water and soil conservation; management of mechanized agricultural enterprises and agro-allied industries; and advanced research. FUNAAB lists teaching/research areas in bio-resources and food process engineering, agricultural power and machinery, water and ecological resources, environmental resources, and agricultural/rural structures.

**Official sources.** [Agricultural and Bio-Resources Engineering department page](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/agricultural-and-bioresources-engineering/); [FUNAAB 2026/2027 admission programme list](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/programmes.php); [Agricultural Engineering course synopsis](https://funaab.edu.ng/age-course-synopsis/); [Agricultural Engineering courses](https://funaab.edu.ng/agricultural-engineering-courses/); [Agricultural Engineering lecture notes](https://funaab.edu.ng/agricultural-engineering-lectures-notes/).

**Direct PDFs.** No programme-specific current undergraduate curriculum PDF was located. A related official College of Engineering postgraduate prospectus PDF is available at [COLENG Postgraduate Prospectus PDF](https://funaab.edu.ng/wp-content/uploads/2010/11/1538_College%20of%20Engineering%20(COLENG)%20%5BPostgraduate%20Prospectus%5D.pdf), but it should not be treated as the undergraduate curriculum.

**Confidence: High.** The synopsis is based on the current official department page and corroborating official admissions/course pages. **Gaps:** no current official undergraduate handbook or consolidated curriculum PDF located; course-synopsis pages may be legacy web content and do not establish current sequencing, prerequisites, or all units.

### Civil Engineering

**Synopsis.** FUNAAB's Civil Engineering programme aims to produce skilled, resourceful, creative and employable engineers grounded in civil-engineering fundamentals and exposed to industry. The stated objectives emphasize development of ideas for practical civil-engineering and agricultural problems, use of appropriate technology, engineering judgment and creativity, and practical preparation through supervised industrial training and site visits. FUNAAB frames likely professional application broadly around infrastructure development and says the qualification is NUC-accredited and registerable with COREN and comparable bodies; that regulatory wording is retained as the university's statement, not independently re-verified here.

**Official sources.** [Civil Engineering department page](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/civil-engineering/); [FUNAAB 2026/2027 admission programme list](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/programmes.php); [Civil Engineering course synopsis](https://funaab.edu.ng/cve-course-synopsis/); [Civil Engineering courses](https://funaab.edu.ng/civil-engineering-courses/); [Civil Engineering lecture notes](https://funaab.edu.ng/civil-engineering-lectures-notes/); [Civil Engineering department page (legacy)](https://funaab.edu.ng/college-of-engineering-coleng/department-civil-engineering/).

**Direct PDFs.** No current programme-specific undergraduate curriculum PDF was located. The official [COLENG Postgraduate Prospectus PDF](https://funaab.edu.ng/wp-content/uploads/2010/11/1538_College%20of%20Engineering%20(COLENG)%20%5BPostgraduate%20Prospectus%5D.pdf) is a postgraduate document and is not a substitute for an undergraduate curriculum.

**Confidence: High.** Current official department description and admission listing are clear, with course-unit evidence from the official synopsis. **Gaps:** no current consolidated undergraduate handbook/curriculum PDF located; the course synopsis does not by itself prove the current complete course list or programme sequence.

### Electrical and Electronics Engineering

**Synopsis.** FUNAAB describes this programme as developing engineers who understand electrical/electronics fundamentals and can generate technologies for national, agricultural and technological advancement. The official description specifically names power systems, renewable energy sources, electronics, automatic control, communications and computer applications, with practicals and supervised projects. Objectives include solving engineering problems using electrical/electronics principles, developing ICT capacity, supporting modern agricultural services, building employability/self-reliance, and supplying manpower for power, telecommunications and industrial sectors.

**Official sources.** [Electrical/Electronics Engineering department page](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/electrical-electronics-engineering/); [FUNAAB 2026/2027 admission programme list](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/programmes.php); [Electrical/Electronics course synopsis](https://funaab.edu.ng/ele-course-synopsis/); [Electrical/Electronics Engineering courses](https://funaab.edu.ng/electricalelectronics-engineering-courses/); [Electrical/Electronics lecture/examination resources](https://funaab.edu.ng/ele-examination-questions/).

**Direct PDFs.** No current programme-specific undergraduate curriculum PDF was located. The official [COLENG Postgraduate Prospectus PDF](https://funaab.edu.ng/wp-content/uploads/2010/11/1538_College%20of%20Engineering%20(COLENG)%20%5BPostgraduate%20Prospectus%5D.pdf) is relevant only as a college-level postgraduate source.

**Confidence: High.** Current official programme description, current admission listing, and detailed official course synopsis align. **Gaps:** no current consolidated undergraduate curriculum PDF located; the course synopsis is a set of unit descriptions rather than a full programme handbook.

### Mechanical Engineering

**Synopsis.** FUNAAB frames Mechanical Engineering around analysis, design and synthesis of systems including prime movers, internal-combustion engines, steam and gas turbines, refrigeration and air-conditioning equipment, machine tools, material-handling systems, industrial production equipment and manufacturing robots. Stated graduate capabilities include reading and preparing engineering drawings, using engineering principles creatively to solve practical problems, installing/operating/maintaining plant and machinery, and applying management skills in engineering organizations. The official course synopsis provides unit-level examples in mechanics, engineering materials, thermodynamics, fluid mechanics, machine drawing/design, manufacturing, measurements and instrumentation, but these are not a substitute for a current curriculum.

**Official sources.** [Mechanical Engineering department page](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/mechanical-engineering/); [FUNAAB 2026/2027 admission programme list](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/programmes.php); [Mechanical Engineering course synopsis](https://funaab.edu.ng/mce-course-synopsis/); [Mechanical Engineering courses](https://funaab.edu.ng/mechanical-engineering-courses/); [Mechanical Engineering lecture notes](https://funaab.edu.ng/mechanical-engineering-lectures-notes/); [Mechanical Engineering accreditation notice](https://funaab.edu.ng/nuc-accreditation-team-arrives-to-evaluate-mechanical-engineering-programme/).

**Direct PDFs.** One official public course-unit PDF was located: [MCE 509 Mechanical Maintenance lecture note PDF](https://funaab.edu.ng/wp-content/uploads/2009/12/470_MCE%20509%20LECTURE%20NOTE.pdf). It is an individual lecture note, not a programme curriculum. No current consolidated undergraduate curriculum PDF was located. The [COLENG Postgraduate Prospectus PDF](https://funaab.edu.ng/wp-content/uploads/2010/11/1538_College%20of%20Engineering%20(COLENG)%20%5BPostgraduate%20Prospectus%5D.pdf) is postgraduate only.

**Confidence: High.** Current official programme philosophy/objectives and course synopsis are detailed and mutually consistent. **Gaps:** current undergraduate handbook, programme map, accreditation curriculum, and prerequisites were not located; the MCE 509 PDF is dated/individual and must not be generalized to the whole degree.

### Mechatronic Engineering

**Synopsis.** FUNAAB describes Mechatronic Engineering as producing graduates with high academic standards and skills to support industrialization, automation, self-reliance and environmental sustainability. The current department objectives cover design of engineering projects, components, machines, equipment and systems; new products and production techniques; industrial management; installation and maintenance of complex systems; adaptation of external technology to local problems; professional judgment and multidisciplinary teamwork. The official course synopsis gives unit-level examples spanning CAD/CAM/CNC, group design projects, control and servomechanisms, heat/mass transfer, instrumentation, computer hardware, power electronics, robotics, PLC/process automation, digital signal modelling, microprocessors/embedded systems, product modelling, mobile robotics and renewable energy.

**Official sources.** [Current Mechatronics Engineering department page](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/mechatronics-engineering/); [legacy department objectives page](https://funaab.edu.ng/department-of-mechatronic-engineering/); [FUNAAB 2026/2027 admission programme list](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/programmes.php); [Mechatronic Engineering course synopsis](https://funaab.edu.ng/course-synopsis-of-mechatronic-engineering/); [Mechatronics final-year project news](https://funaab.edu.ng/mechatronics-engineering-department-showcases-innovative-final-year-projects-at-funaab/).

**Direct PDFs.** No current programme-specific undergraduate curriculum PDF was located. The official [COLENG Postgraduate Prospectus PDF](https://funaab.edu.ng/wp-content/uploads/2010/11/1538_College%20of%20Engineering%20(COLENG)%20%5BPostgraduate%20Prospectus%5D.pdf) is not a Mechatronics undergraduate curriculum. The official course-synopsis page is HTML, not a direct PDF.

**Confidence: High.** Current department page and current admission listing directly establish the programme, while the course synopsis provides corroborating unit-level scope. **Gaps:** no current consolidated undergraduate curriculum PDF or handbook located; the legacy department page uses some mechanical-engineering wording, so the current section page is preferred for programme-level claims.

## Source and interpretation notes

The report distinguishes programme-level claims (philosophy, objectives, graduate capabilities and stated application areas) from course-unit claims (topics attached to named course codes). It does not infer a course from a department's research interest, staff profile, lecture-note title, or an isolated unit synopsis. Where FUNAAB's pages use variant names—Agricultural and Bio-Resources Engineering; Electrical/Electronics Engineering; Mechatronics versus Mechatronic Engineering—the admission page's programme names are retained for catalogue consistency, while the official department wording is quoted or paraphrased faithfully. No secondary student guides were used.

**Access date:** research performed 13 September 2026 (sandbox date). Web pages may change; catalogue maintainers should recheck current admission and curriculum pages before publication.

## High-level gaps for an AI tutor catalogue

The public sources do not provide a single current undergraduate curriculum document with semester sequencing, prerequisites, unit weights for every year, learning outcomes by course, assessment methods, or current accreditation version. Consequently, an AI tutor should use the programme synopses as broad orientation only and should not claim that every named course is currently offered, compulsory, or scheduled in a particular semester without a current departmental handbook or official curriculum approval document.
