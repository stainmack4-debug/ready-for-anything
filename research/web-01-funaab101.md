# FUNAAB 101 site-group research report

**Research ID:** `web-01-funaab101`  
**Scope:** Public pages reachable from [FUNAAB 101](https://www.funaab101.xyz/) and selected official FUNAAB corroboration.  
**Status:** Public, unauthenticated web review; no login, paywall, robots restriction, or access-control bypass was attempted.  
**Research date:** 2026-09-13.

## Executive conclusion

[FUNAAB 101](https://www.funaab101.xyz/) is an **independent student guide**, not an official Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta (FUNAAB) publication. Its home page explicitly says that it organizes student-facing information and that fees, deadlines, and official decisions must be verified with the university [1]. The site provides a useful navigation layer for academics, freshers, resources, tools, past questions, course materials, colleges, department groups, and the Students’ Union Government (SUG), but the crawl found **no publicly linked PDF file** hosted by `funaab101.xyz`. The resource pages describe past questions and course materials as available or growing collections, yet the observed public pages did not expose downloadable question papers, lecture notes, course synopses, or direct PDF URLs.

Official university pages therefore rank higher for authoritative academic content. In particular, FUNAAB’s official Physics synopsis provides detailed course descriptions, and its official open-courseware file provides a direct PHS 101 PDF URL [17] [18]. These official materials were used only as corroboration and are not attributed to FUNAAB 101.

## Source ranking and method

| Rank | Source class | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Official `funaab.edu.ng` pages and files | Highest authority for curricula, course titles, synopses, calendars, and institutional decisions. |
| 2 | FUNAAB 101 pages that link or point to official sources | Useful student-oriented index; verify the underlying official source. |
| 3 | FUNAAB 101 original guidance and group listings | Independent student information; useful for orientation and discovery, but not an official university record. |

The site was crawled from the stated start URL, following same-host HTTP(S) links, removing URL fragments, and deduplicating URLs. Pages returning HTML were inspected for visible text and links. A linked `.pdf` check was also performed. The result was 39 HTML responses before URL normalization exposed repeated home-page variants; the report lists canonical paths rather than repeated aliases. A few links point to `.html` variants or to missing pages, so those were recorded as caveats rather than treated as separate substantive resources.

## Academic guide and calendar

The Academics page names these colleges: COLPLANT, COLANIM, COLERM, COLVET, COLENG, COLBIOS, COLPHYS, COLEND, COLFHEC, COLCOMPS, and COLAMRUD. It cautions that college and department structures can change and advises students to confirm their exact department and course adviser with the college secretariat [2].

The same page presents a “2026/2027 official calendar” summary. The visible entries include fresh-student registration, medical screening, ICT training and orientation beginning **7 September 2026**, first-semester lectures beginning **21 September 2026**, matriculation on **18 November 2026**, first-semester CAT beginning **2 December 2026**, CAT ending **17 December 2026**, and lectures ending **18 December 2026** [2]. Because this is a student-guide transcription, the university’s current calendar should control if dates differ. The page also gives general study advice: track courses and assessment structure, track CATs/assignments/practicals, and build a revision bank before examinations.

## Course names, descriptions, and synopses

### What FUNAAB 101 itself exposes

The FUNAAB 101 crawl found **no course-by-course names with descriptions or synopses** on the public `/course-materials` and `/past-questions` landing pages. Those pages advertise or organize resources but did not expose downloadable course files or visible synopsis text during this review. Accordingly, no FUNAAB 101 course synopsis is invented here.

## Past questions and course-material findings

FUNAAB 101’s [Past Questions](https://www.funaab101.xyz/past-questions) page is a revision-resource landing page. The [Course Materials](https://www.funaab101.xyz/course-materials) page is a landing page for lecture notes, handouts, and other student-shared resources. The home page describes these collections as resources that can grow over time [1]. During the public crawl, neither page exposed a direct PDF, a visible file-download URL, or a course-by-course synopsis. No login or access-control mechanism was bypassed.

The [Resources](https://www.funaab101.xyz/resources) page also links to the university’s official site and admission portal. That is a positive source-ranking signal: FUNAAB 101 presents itself as an organizer and points readers to official sources for decisions. It does not make the student guide an official university repository.

## Direct PDF URLs

### PDFs hosted or linked by the FUNAAB 101 site group

**None found in the public crawl.** The site has PDF-processing utilities, but the tools do not constitute course PDFs. The academic page exposes an image-style asset named `academic calendar.png`, not a PDF. No `.pdf` URL was linked from the public site-group pages inspected.

### Official FUNAAB PDF found for corroboration

The following is an official university PDF discovered during source verification, not a PDF hosted by or attributed to FUNAAB 101:

- [PHS 101 General Physics I course outline PDF](https://funaab.edu.ng/funaab-ocw/opencourseware/PHS%20101.pdf) [18]. The indexed outline covers mechanics topics including units and dimensions, rectilinear motion, Newton’s laws, friction, work, energy, and conservation of energy.

## Quality notes and limitations

The independent-site label is central: FUNAAB 101 should be used as a student navigation aid, while official FUNAAB pages control institutional facts. The site includes dynamic or changing information, including dates, office-holders, group destinations, and programme labels. Several same-content URL forms (`/`, `/index`, `.html` variants) were deduplicated. `/admission` and `/opportunities` returned a page-not-found response, so they are not treated as live resources. Some group links point to external destinations whose content was not treated as part of this exact site group. No claims were made about content hidden behind login, uploads, robots restrictions, or access controls. Search-engine results were used only to locate official corroboration and were followed by fetching the official pages.
