# FUNAAB Web Resource Index

**Prepared from:** web-01-funaab101, web-02-official, and web-03-other research results  
**Scope:** Public, unauthenticated web resources discovered for the Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta (FUNAAB).  
**Status:** Consolidated index; URLs and descriptions reflect the supplied crawl results and are not a substitute for checking the live university site.

## Executive summary

The strongest sources are the university’s official `funaab.edu.ng` pages and official subdomains. These include OpenCourseWare, course-synopsis pages, admission notices, postgraduate information, examination-question indexes, academic calendars, and the institutional repository. The `funaab101.xyz` site is useful as an independent student guide, but it explicitly advises readers to verify fees, deadlines, and official decisions with FUNAAB. CropPSA notes, Scholarly Africa, and other third-party or independently maintained pages should be treated as discovery aids rather than authoritative institutional records.

The crawl identified **20 distinct direct PDF URLs** after deduplication. Only the official FUNAAB PHS 101 OpenCourseWare PDF was found as a direct course PDF through the FUNAAB 101 corroboration; the other direct PDFs came from official university, admission, postgraduate, policy, calendar, or courseware locations. No direct PDF hosted or linked by `funaab101.xyz` was exposed by its public course-materials or past-questions landing pages.

The synopsis index below contains **31 unique course entries**. It combines detailed official synopses with official course-title listings for which no description was exposed in the fetched public page. Confidence is stated per entry so that title evidence is not mistaken for a full course synopsis.

## Warnings and verification guidance

> **Unofficial material warning.** FUNAAB 101 identifies itself as an independent student resource. It is useful for navigation and student orientation, but it is not the authority for fees, deadlines, admissions decisions, academic calendars, course approval, or other official university decisions. Verify those matters on official FUNAAB or admission pages [6].

> **Unverified third-party warning.** CropPSA, Scholarly Africa, and similar independently discoverable pages may contain useful notes or practice materials, but their authorship, completeness, currency, copyright status, and institutional approval were not established. Do not use them as the sole basis for academic or administrative decisions.

> **Archival-PDF warning.** Several official PDFs are old examination papers, lecture notes, calendars, or policy files. An official host confirms provenance of the URL, not that the document remains current. Check the academic session, revision number, and current university notice before relying on it.

> **Title-versus-synopsis warning.** An official course list can support a course title without providing a synopsis. The entries marked **Title only** should not be expanded into assumed learning outcomes without consulting the department or current course handbook.

> **No-access-bypass statement.** The research used public, unauthenticated pages only. No login, paywall, robots rule, upload, or access-control bypass was attempted. Sparse pages, embedded links, and resources requiring authentication may therefore be absent.

> **No invented URLs.** Direct PDF links were included only when exposed by the supplied research. Third-party search results such as Scribd, PDFCoffee, and unrelated document hosts were not promoted to verified FUNAAB resources.

## Recommended verification workflow

For admissions, registration, fees, deadlines, and official decisions, begin with the relevant official FUNAAB or admission-portal page and confirm the academic session. For course content, use the official course-synopsis, OpenCourseWare, lecture-note, or department page before consulting independent notes. For past questions, treat dates and course codes as historical metadata rather than evidence of the current examination format. For postgraduate courses, verify the department’s current programme page because course outlines and requirements can change between sessions.
