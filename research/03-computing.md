# FUNAAB Academic Domain Research: Computing and Information Systems

**Scope.** This report covers the seven undergraduate programmes listed by the Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta (FUNAAB) under the 2026/2027 admission listing’s **Computing Science** grouping: Computer Science, Cyber Security, Data Science, Information Communication Technology, Information Systems, Information Technology, and Software Engineering. The descriptions below are programme-level synopses, not individual course-unit syllabi. Where the official site provides rationale/objectives, those statements are summarized conservatively without adding unverified course content.

## Institutional and programme evidence

FUNAAB’s official 2026/2027 pre-admission programme page lists all seven programmes under “Computing Science,” each with a stated minimum acceptable UTME score of 200. The University’s 28 September 2024 announcement records NUC approval for six new computing programmes—Data Science, Cyber Security, Information Systems, Software Engineering, Information and Communication Technology, and Information Technology—commencing in 2024/2025; Computer Science is the established programme. FUNAAB’s College of Computing Sciences page frames computing training around skilled manpower, practical/laboratory instruction, current technologies, analytical ability, communication, entrepreneurship, and lifelong learning.

## Programme catalogue

### 1. Computer Science

**Synopsis.** FUNAAB describes the programme as a broad education in both the theory and practice of computer science. Its stated objectives include sustained interest in computing and applications; programming for real-life problems; preparation for computing services in industry, banking, and other sectors; development of internationally acceptable software applications; basic network and hardware knowledge; and preparation for postgraduate study and research. This is a broad computer-science programme, rather than a claim about a particular specialization.

**Official sources.** [Department of Computer Science](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/department-of-computer-science/); [2026/2027 programme listing](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/programmes.php); [College of Computing Sciences](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/college-of-computing-sciences/).

**PDF sources.** None located that functions as a current Computer Science curriculum or handbook. FUNAAB’s historical [Computer Science Courses page](https://funaab.edu.ng/computer-science-courses/) lists legacy course titles (last updated 2011), but it is not treated as a current curriculum or as evidence that every listed unit remains in the programme.

**Confidence.** High for the programme-level synopsis; medium for current course-level detail.

**Gaps.** No current official curriculum, semester-by-semester course map, credit structure, or individual course-unit syllabi were located.

### 2. Cyber Security

**Synopsis.** FUNAAB’s stated philosophy is to develop graduates able to protect digital assets, critical infrastructure, and business ecosystems. The objectives cover foundational cybersecurity knowledge; analysis of organizational threats and risks; detection and prevention of cyber-fraud; security-supporting code and software agents; digital forensics; cryptography; employment/self-employment; and professional lifelong learning. The official rationale additionally identifies big-data analytics, cyber-threat intelligence and conflict, deep/dark-web security, threat hunting, artificial-intelligence cyber defence, and surveillance in cyber-defence operations. These are official programme rationale areas, not a verified list of course units.

**Official sources.** [Department of Cyber Security and Data Science](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/department-of-cyber-security-and-data-science/); [NUC approval announcement](https://funaab.edu.ng/nuc-approves-eight-new-programmes-for-funaab/); [2026/2027 programme listing](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/programmes.php).

**PDF sources.** [2026 FUNAAB Admission Requirements PDF](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/files/FUNAABAdmissionRequirements.pdf) (official admission requirements; not a curriculum).

**Confidence.** High for the programme-level synopsis.

**Gaps.** No official public curriculum/handbook or course-unit syllabi located; exact sequencing, credits, laboratory requirements, and accreditation-cycle details remain unverified.

### 3. Data Science

**Synopsis.** FUNAAB’s programme philosophy is to produce graduates who can reveal hidden information and knowledge in very large data stores to guide decisions and create organizational value. Objectives emphasize computationally intensive real-life problem solving; selecting and applying techniques to extract relevant information; data engineering, big data, and analytics; and integrating data-driven IT solutions with business processes. The official rationale highlights hands-on data analytics, machine learning, artificial intelligence, and deep learning, together with problem solving, research, communication, collaboration, critical thinking, information analysis, and responsible digital citizenship.

**Official sources.** [Department of Cyber Security and Data Science](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/department-of-cyber-security-and-data-science/); [NUC approval announcement](https://funaab.edu.ng/nuc-approves-eight-new-programmes-for-funaab/); [2026/2027 programme listing](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/programmes.php).

**PDF sources.** [2026 FUNAAB Admission Requirements PDF](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/files/FUNAABAdmissionRequirements.pdf) (official admission requirements; not a curriculum).

**Confidence.** High for the programme-level synopsis.

**Gaps.** No current official curriculum, course map, credit structure, or public course-unit syllabi located. The official page does not establish which programming languages, software platforms, datasets, or assessment formats are used.

### 4. Information Communication Technology

**Synopsis.** FUNAAB presents ICT as preparation for self-management and professional development, enabling seamless ICT integration for productivity using professional standards and best practices. Objectives cover foundational ICT concepts and skills, acquisition and disposal of ICTs, professional ICT practice, awareness of emerging technologies, and practical ICT utilization. The official rationale identifies hands-on components and a target profile involving web applications, multimedia, document processing, mobile computing, and internet communications, with career directions in ICT commercialisation and technology management.

**Official sources.** [Department of Information Technology and Information Communication Technology](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/department-of-information-technology-and-information-communication-technology/); [NUC approval announcement](https://funaab.edu.ng/nuc-approves-eight-new-programmes-for-funaab/); [2026/2027 programme listing](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/programmes.php).

**PDF sources.** [FUNAAB ICT Policy PDF](https://funaab.edu.ng/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/Funnabictpolicy.pdf) (institutional ICT policy, not an undergraduate curriculum); [2026 FUNAAB Admission Requirements PDF](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/files/FUNAABAdmissionRequirements.pdf) (admission requirements, not a curriculum).

**Confidence.** High for the programme-level synopsis.

**Gaps.** No current public curriculum/handbook or individual ICT course syllabi located. The institutional ICT Policy should not be used to infer programme course content.

### 5. Information Systems

**Synopsis.** FUNAAB’s stated philosophy focuses on producing graduates able to develop and maintain quality information systems at scale for organizations, governments, industries, and businesses while meeting functional and non-functional requirements within time and budget constraints. Objectives specify foundations in problem solving, algorithms, data structures, and programming; practical requirements analysis, system design, architecture, metrics, verification and validation; programming in multiple languages; robust, reliable, cost-effective and secure systems; and management of large, complex, critical systems. The department’s vision also emphasizes information education, research, innovation, and professionals who drive technological advancement.

**Official sources.** [Department of Software Engineering and Information Systems](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/department-of-software-engineering-and-information-system/); [NUC approval announcement](https://funaab.edu.ng/nuc-approves-eight-new-programmes-for-funaab/); [2026/2027 programme listing](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/programmes.php).

**PDF sources.** [2026 FUNAAB Admission Requirements PDF](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/files/FUNAABAdmissionRequirements.pdf) (official admission requirements; not a curriculum).

**Confidence.** High for the programme-level synopsis.

**Gaps.** The public department page closely parallels the Software Engineering objectives and does not publish a detailed Information Systems curriculum, course map, or syllabi. Distinctive Information Systems course content should therefore not be inferred from the page.

### 6. Information Technology

**Synopsis.** FUNAAB positions Information Technology around solving global needs through IT solutions, with information assurance and security, abstraction/modeling, and professional standards. Objectives cover IT foundations and practice; creating and applying IT for data management and practical problems; professional computing and communications; IT infrastructure and quality improvement; employment and self-employment; and further academic/professional development. The official rationale identifies hands-on work in web technologies, network servers and infrastructure, mobile/pervasive computing, and mobile/web application development.

**Official sources.** [Department of Information Technology and Information Communication Technology](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/department-of-information-technology-and-information-communication-technology/); [NUC approval announcement](https://funaab.edu.ng/nuc-approves-eight-new-programmes-for-funaab/); [2026/2027 programme listing](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/programmes.php).

**PDF sources.** [2026 FUNAAB Admission Requirements PDF](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/files/FUNAABAdmissionRequirements.pdf) (official admission requirements; not a curriculum); [FUNAAB ICT Policy PDF](https://funaab.edu.ng/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/Funnabictpolicy.pdf) (institutional policy, not a curriculum).

**Confidence.** High for the programme-level synopsis.

**Gaps.** No current official curriculum, course-unit syllabi, credit structure, or detailed infrastructure laboratory specification located.

### 7. Software Engineering

**Synopsis.** FUNAAB’s philosophy is to produce graduates able to develop and maintain quality software systems at scale for industry, government, organizations, and businesses, satisfying functional and non-functional requirements within time and budget constraints. Objectives cover computing foundations; requirements analysis; system design and architecture; metrics; verification and validation; software processes; programming in multiple languages; robust, reliable, cost-effective and secure systems; management of large, complex and critical software; ethical professional practice; and self-employment/job creation.

**Official sources.** [Department of Software Engineering and Information Systems](https://funaab.edu.ng/section/department-of-software-engineering-and-information-system/); [NUC approval announcement](https://funaab.edu.ng/nuc-approves-eight-new-programmes-for-funaab/); [2026/2027 programme listing](https://admission.funaab.edu.ng/2026/programmes.php).

## Source and confidence notes

All programme synopses rely primarily on official FUNAAB department pages, the official admission programme listing, the official NUC approval announcement, and official FUNAAB-hosted PDFs. No secondary student guide was needed. The historical Computer Science course page is explicitly treated as legacy course-list evidence, not as a current curriculum. No publicly accessible, current, programme-specific curriculum or course-unit syllabus set was found for the seven programmes during this search. Therefore, an AI tutor catalogue should use the synopses as high-confidence programme-level descriptions while avoiding claims about exact modules, semester placement, credits, laboratory tools, programming languages, or learning outcomes beyond those stated by FUNAAB.
