# FUNAAB Academic Domain Research: Agriculture and Crop/Animal Production

**Research scope.** This catalogue covers exactly the 23 programmes listed under FUNAAB in the official 2026/2027 pre-admission screening page: Agriculture; Agricultural Administration; Agricultural Economics and Farm Management; Agricultural Extension and Rural Development; Animal Breeding and Genetics; Animal Nutrition; Animal Physiology; Animal Production and Health; Aquaculture and Fisheries Management; Climate Science and Agricultural Meteorology; Crop Protection; Environmental Management and Toxicology; Forest Resource Management; Geology; Horticulture; Hydrology and Water Resources Management; Pasture and Range Management; Plant Breeding and Seed Technology; Plant Physiology and Crop Production; Soil Science and Land Management; Water Resources Management and Agro-meteorology; Wildlife and Eco-tourism Management; and Water Sanitation and Hygiene [1].

The official FUNAAB material is uneven. The university publishes programme lists, college/department descriptions, selected undergraduate pages, postgraduate specializations, course-unit lists, and several PDFs. It does **not** expose a complete, current course handbook or individual syllabus for every listed undergraduate programme in the sources located for this report. Therefore, each synopsis below is deliberately limited to statements supported by programme-level material. A course list is not treated as a programme description.

## Source and evidence notes

The official admission page is the controlling source for the requested set of 23 programme names [1]. COLAMRUD confirms the three management/rural-development departments and describes the B.Agric options [3]. COLANIM confirms the five animal-science departments, their contribution to the B.Agric curriculum, common early-level courses, and Farm Practical Year [2]. COLPLANT confirms the five crop/soil departments and its broad training aims [13]. The environmental, geology, forestry/wildlife, soil, and water-resource pages supply programme-level descriptions [15] [17] [16] [18] [12]. The postgraduate programme page supplies specializations and should be read as evidence of departmental subject areas, not as a substitute for an undergraduate syllabus [9].

The Agricultural Extension page lists individual course units such as principles and practice of extension, extension teaching methods, agricultural communication, rural community development, and extension administration [7]. It is explicitly treated here as a course-unit source. The Water Resources and Agrometeorology page also contains individual M.Sc. course synopses, including micrometeorology and agro-hydrology [19]. Those details are not generalized into all undergraduate programmes.

## Research gaps and catalogue policy

No secondary student guide was needed for the synopses above. The principal gap is the absence of a single current, public, programme-by-programme undergraduate handbook covering all 23 admission-listed programmes. In particular, Water Sanitation and Hygiene has no located official programme-level description, so its confidence is low and the catalogue should not generate specific course claims for it. For all other entries, the synopsis is a bounded programme-level description. Individual course-unit titles or postgraduate specializations must not be presented to learners as the complete undergraduate curriculum.
